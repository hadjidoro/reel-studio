## Shape
- **15–25 s**, 4–6 scenes. Viewers decide in about 1.5 s whether to keep watching, so frame 0 must already show the hook.
- The **hook** is a question or tension the viewer recognises: "Your car sits in the garage?" or "300 000 F a month? Here's the real math."
- **One idea per scene.** Scenes have at most 12 words of headline, plus an optional short sub-line.
- Show text long enough to read twice, about 0.35 s per word, and never less than 2.5 s per scene.
- **End with one action:** visit the site, comment, save, or share with a friend. Social platforms reward saves and shares, so ask for them.

## Formats that work for product and marketplace pages
| Format | Scene recipe |
|---|---|
| Problem → product demo | hook, phone (3 steps), chat or statement, end |
| The real math | number, calc, facts, cta |
| Checklist | hook, list (✓), cta ("save this") |
| Red flags / myths | hook, list (✕), statement |
| Versus | versus, facts A, facts B, statement ("comment 👇") |
| N golden rules | hook, rule ×3, cta |
| Coverage / local | hook, chips, statement |
| Before / after | statement (before), statement (after), cta |
| FAQ | hook ("You asked…"), facts or statement per answer, cta |

## Series & cadence
- Alternate audiences and formats. Post 3 a week, and reuse the best-performing format with a new topic.
- Number the series in the caption or kicker to build habit, for example "Tip #4".

## Captions
- The first line repeats the hook, with 1–2 emoji at most.
- The second line gives the benefit and a call to action with the URL.
- Add 5–8 hashtags: city, niche, product and brand.
- Write in the language and formality level of `context.md`.

## Accuracy
- Every figure comes from `context.md`. Show a small source note on money and calculation scenes.
- Flag time-sensitive facts to the user: prices, commissions, legal dates and competitor status.
- Mock screens only. Use invented names, and mask any phone numbers (07 •• •• ••).
